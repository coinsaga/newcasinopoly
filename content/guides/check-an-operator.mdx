Choosing a betting operator starts with checking who runs it and what it is authorised to offer. A familiar logo, a price shown in rand or a South African flag on a website does not establish local authorisation.

## Start with the licence and the product

The National Gambling Board (NGB) distinguishes prohibited online gambling from online betting through bookmakers licensed in South Africa. An overseas licence should not be treated as proof that a site or its games are authorised locally. Read the [NGB's current enforcement guidance](https://www.ngb.org.za/industry-regulation/enforcement/) before deciding to register.

Look for the operator's legal entity, licence number and provincial licensing authority. Compare those details with the [NGB's verified operators list](https://www.ngb.org.za/verified-operators/). Match the entity and website address, rather than relying on a similar trading name. If the scope of a product or licence is unclear, ask the relevant licensing authority before using it.

## Check the account and payment conditions

Before sharing identity documents or paying money, read the operator's own terms and privacy notice. Check:

- Whether the specific service is available to you in South Africa.
- Which identity and address documents the operator requires, and how they must be submitted.
- Whether your account and transactions use ZAR, including any currency conversion or payment fees.
- Which deposit and withdrawal methods are actually available to your account, including limits and processing conditions.
- How to contact support and follow the operator's complaints procedure.

A deposit method does not automatically support withdrawals. Check the withdrawal terms separately and make sure the account holder details can be matched to your own.

## Set boundaries before opening an account

Gambling is for adults aged 18 and over. Decide on a spending limit you can afford to lose and a time limit before you start. Look for deposit limits, time-out options and self-exclusion information in the operator's responsible gambling section. Gambling should never be used to pay bills or recover previous losses.

You do not need to accept an offer to assess an operator. If you are considering one, use our guide to [reading offer terms](/guides/understand-offer-terms) first.

## Sources and checks

The NGB enforcement guidance and verified operators list linked above were checked on 20 October 2026. These sources are a starting point for checking an operator; the current licence and the authorisation for the specific product still need to be confirmed. Terms and licensing information can change.
