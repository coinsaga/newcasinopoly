An offer's headline does not tell you what you can withdraw. Before opting in, read the full terms on the operator's website and decide whether the conditions fit a budget you have already set.

## Confirm eligibility first

Check that the offer applies to your location, account and chosen product. New-customer conditions, age requirements, verification steps and restrictions on multiple accounts can affect eligibility. If an offer is unclear, ask support to explain it before depositing.

An offer does not establish that an operator or product is authorised in South Africa. Start with our guide to [checking an operator](/guides/check-an-operator), including the official licensing sources it links to.

## Separate your deposit from promotional funds

Check the minimum qualifying deposit in rand and whether the displayed amount is cash, bonus credit or a free bet. Read how winnings are calculated and whether a free-bet stake is returned. A promotion may also exclude a payment method that the operator otherwise accepts for deposits.

For percentage offers, a maximum bonus is a cap, rather than an amount everyone receives. Avoid depositing more than you intended just to reach it.

## Understand the turnover calculation

Wagering or turnover requirements specify how much qualifying activity is needed before certain funds can be withdrawn. Check what the multiplier applies to: the bonus, your deposit plus the bonus, or another stated amount.

For a purely illustrative example, a R100 bonus with a 5× bonus-only turnover requirement would require R500 in qualifying stakes. That calculation does not mean R500 is a guaranteed cost, a profit or a safe amount to spend. Minimum odds, excluded bets, contribution rules and deadlines can change what counts. This example is not an offer from any operator.

## Read the restrictions together

Before claiming, locate the terms covering:

- The deadline to opt in, use the offer and complete any turnover.
- Minimum odds, eligible markets and how voided or cashed-out bets are treated.
- Any maximum qualifying stake, winnings cap or withdrawal limit.
- What happens if you withdraw your own money or cancel the promotion early.
- Verification requirements and the complaints process if a promotion is disputed.

Keep a dated copy of the terms that applied when you opted in. If a term is missing or contradictory, seek clarification before taking part.

## An offer is optional

You can decline a promotion. Do not borrow money, increase your budget or chase losses to complete a requirement. If gambling is becoming hard to control, stop and use the account's time-out or self-exclusion options, and seek support.
